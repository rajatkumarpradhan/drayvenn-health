About Drayvenn Health

Drayvenn Health is Drayvenn Lab's clinic appointment booking platform. It exists to make seeing a neighbourhood doctor as easy as sending a message: pick a clinic, pick a time, walk in. No online payment, no phone calls to the front desk - and every booking stays in one account.

What we are building

  • Instant online booking for local clinics, across Bengaluru
  • A simple dashboard where clinics confirm and manage their day's appointments
  • A 24x7 assistant that helps with booking questions (never medical advice)

Who is behind it

Drayvenn Health is built and operated by Drayvenn Lab, a small software studio in Bengaluru. The clinics listed in this demo build are sample clinics that show how the platform works; real clinics come on board at launch.
