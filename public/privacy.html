Privacy Policy

Drayvenn Health is a clinic appointment booking service by Drayvenn Lab, Bengaluru. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to have it deleted. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Patient name and age
  • Mobile number used for the booking
  • Appointment details - clinic, doctor, date and time slot
  • A short concern note you choose to write (optional)

What we never collect

  • No diagnosis, prescriptions, reports or medical records
  • No passwords or accounts for patients
  • No payment details - you pay at the clinic

How your data is used

  • To create and manage your appointment with the clinic you booked
  • The clinic you booked sees your name, age, mobile number and concern so they can confirm and run your visit
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone else

Who sees your data

Only the clinic you booked with sees your details. The Drayvenn Lab team accesses data only to run and fix the service. We never sell data.

How long we keep it

Booking data is automatically deleted 1 year after the appointment date. If you ask us to delete it earlier (form below), we action the request within 7 days and confirm.

Your choices

  • See or cancel a booking anytime from the Bookings page with your reference and mobile number
  • Ask us to delete your booking data using the form below - we action it within 7 days and confirm
