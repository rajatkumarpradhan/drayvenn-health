What Drayvenn Lab is

Drayvenn Lab is an appointment booking platform. We are not a hospital, clinic or healthcare provider, and we do not employ the doctors listed. The medical service itself is provided only by the clinic and its doctors, and they are responsible for it.

No medical advice, emergencies

Nothing on this site, including the chat assistant, is medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. The assistant can only suggest what type of specialist to see. For any health concern, speak to a qualified doctor. In an emergency call 108 or 112, or go to the nearest hospital. Do not wait for an appointment.

Bookings

  • A booking on Drayvenn Lab is a request. It becomes final only when the clinic confirms it. Until then it shows as Pending.
  • The clinic can reject or reschedule a request, for example if the doctor is unavailable. We will show the new status in your bookings.
  • Rescheduling a booking sets it back to Pending until the clinic confirms the new time.
  • Each booking gets a reference. Keep it private. With your mobile number, it is what protects your booking details.
  • When you book for someone else, you confirm you are allowed to share their details and to consent for them.

Fees and payment

Consultation fees shown are set by each clinic and may change. You pay the clinic directly at the clinic. Drayvenn Lab does not collect payment for consultations and charges patients nothing to book.

Cancelling and no-shows

You can cancel a Pending booking yourself from your bookings or the Track page. For a booking the clinic has already confirmed, please call the clinic to cancel. Please cancel rather than miss an appointment, so the slot goes to another patient. Clinics may have their own no-show rules.

Your responsibilities

  • Give correct details (name, age, mobile number) so the clinic can reach you.
  • Do not book under someone else’s identity, make fake or repeated bookings, or use another person’s booking reference.
  • Do not scrape, probe, overload or try to break into the service. Guessing of booking references is rate-limited and blocked.
  • You must be 18 or older to book for yourself. A parent or guardian must book for a child.

Some clinics shown while we are building the service may be marked as sample clinics, and bookings on them are not real appointments. Listing on Drayvenn Lab is not an endorsement or a guarantee of quality.
