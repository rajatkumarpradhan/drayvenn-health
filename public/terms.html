What Drayvenn Lab is

Drayvenn Lab helps you find neighbourhood clinics and request appointment slots online. We are a booking platform - we are not a hospital, clinic, or healthcare provider, and we do not employ the doctors listed.

No medical advice

Nothing on this site or in the chat assistant is medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. For any health concern, consult a qualified doctor. In an emergency call 108 or 112 immediately - do not wait for an appointment.

Bookings

  • A booking on Drayvenn Lab is a request. The clinic confirms your slot shortly after; the appointment is final only once the clinic confirms it.
  • Consultation fees shown are the clinic's own prices. You pay the clinic directly - Drayvenn Lab never takes payment.
  • Keep your booking reference private. It, together with your mobile number, is what protects your booking details.
  • You can cancel an upcoming appointment yourself from the Track booking page. Please cancel rather than not show up - it frees the slot for another patient.

Your responsibilities

  • Enter accurate details (name, age, mobile number) so the clinic can reach you.
  • Do not book under someone else's identity or misuse another person's booking reference.
  • Do not attempt to scrape, probe, or disrupt the service. Automated guessing of booking references is rate-limited and blocked.
